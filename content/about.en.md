Hello! I’m **Igor Ponce**, an airline pilot, entrepreneur, and technology enthusiast.

My journey has always been driven by curiosity, a desire to keep learning, and the ambition to turn ideas into real projects. Today, I work as an **airline pilot**, flying the Airbus A350 around the world, and I am also the founder of **Saga Virtual**, a company focused on developing technology solutions for aviation, education, and management.

Throughout my career, I have had the opportunity to work as a pilot, instructor, recruiter, training specialist, and digital product developer. These experiences have allowed me to explore different sides of aviation, from a pilot’s initial training to commercial airline operations, including safety, training, technology, and management.

## Why did I create this blog?

I created this space to document and share experiences, lessons, and reflections on the topics that are part of my life:

* **Aviation:** pilot training, career development, operational safety, training, and insights into the profession;
* **Technology:** software development, artificial intelligence, automation, infrastructure, and digital product creation;
* **Career:** professional choices, moving to different countries, challenges, opportunities, and continuous development;
* **Entrepreneurship:** building companies, managing teams, creating solutions, and turning ideas into businesses;
* **Motivation:** discipline, resilience, decision-making, and the pursuit of a life with greater purpose and freedom.

## Between aviation and technology

To me, aviation and technology have a great deal in common. Both fields require planning, precision, continuous learning, and the ability to make good decisions, even in complex environments.

While aviation has taught me about responsibility, discipline, teamwork, and risk management, technology has shown me that it is possible to create solutions that improve processes, solve real problems, and positively impact thousands of people.

Many of the projects I have developed over the years were born from this combination.

## A journey still in progress

I do not write as someone who has all the answers. I write as someone who is still learning, testing ideas, making mistakes, navigating change, and constantly seeking to grow.

My goal is to share honest and useful content, showing not only the results, but also the challenges, doubts, and decisions that are part of every professional and personal journey.

I hope the articles published here can help those who are beginning a career in aviation, developing a project, building a career, or simply looking for motivation to keep moving forward.

> The world is as big as we choose to make it.
